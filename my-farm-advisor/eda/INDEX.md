# EDA Index

Open these guides when analyzing agricultural datasets, comparing conditions, or building visuals.

- [Explore Guide](eda-explore/GUIDE.md) - first-pass inspection and summaries
- [Compare Guide](eda-compare/GUIDE.md) - grouped and category comparisons
- [Correlate Guide](eda-correlate/GUIDE.md) - pairwise and multivariate relationships
- [Time Series Guide](eda-time-series/GUIDE.md) - seasonal and longitudinal views
- [Visualize Guide](eda-visualize/GUIDE.md) - charts, plots, and dashboards
- [Field Comparison](eda-field-comparison/GUIDE.md) - runnable multi-grower EDA across field boundaries, CDL/cropland and weather (within-field, within-grower, cross-grower)
- [Field-Season Dashboard](eda-field-season-dashboard/GUIDE.md) - runnable one-field field-year dashboard aligning Sentinel-2 NDVI, daily weather, CDL crop and cumulative GDD on one growing-season axis
